Two weeks of private, one-to-one training that takes you from your first
correct cut to the standard a professional sushi counter demands.

**Start dates.** Training is on demand and arranged individually. Contact us
to agree a start date.

## Who trains with us

Because you train alone with your instructor, the program suits very
different starting points: beginners and aspiring sushi chefs building a
precise foundation from day one; professional chefs and experienced cooks
adding authentic sushi technique to their repertoire; restaurant owners
and entrepreneurs preparing to launch or improve a sushi offering;
hospitality professionals developing a specialist skill; and private
students learning the craft to a professional standard, for the love of
it.

## Why one-to-one

- **Your pace, your goals.** No class to keep up with and none to wait
  for. Beginners get the time they need on the fundamentals; experienced
  cooks move straight into advanced technique.
- **A curriculum built for you.** We shape the content around what you
  want to do with it, whether that's a restaurant menu, a career move, or
  mastery for its own sake.
- **Correction as you work.** Your instructor sees every cut you make and
  fixes technique in the moment, before mistakes become habits.
- **Difficulty that follows your skill.** As your hands improve, the work
  gets harder. You are never left repeating what you already know.

## What you will master

- **Sushi fundamentals** — preparing and seasoning sushi rice; preparing,
  cutting and portioning fish.
- **The classic repertoire** — nigiri-zushi, hosomaki, futomaki, uramaki,
  temaki, gunkan-maki, sashimi.
- **Craft and creativity** — traditional and modern techniques, sauces and
  toppings, presentation and plating, sushi menu development, professional
  kitchen workflow.
- **Professional knife skills** — knife handling and safety, core cutting
  techniques, fish portioning, slicing for sashimi, cutting for nigiri and
  maki, knife maintenance and sharpening basics.
- **Hygiene & food safety** — personal and kitchen hygiene, preventing
  cross-contamination, safe handling of raw fish, temperature control and
  food storage, cleaning and sanitation, HACCP principles.
- **Professional kitchen skills** — mise en place and sushi station
  organization, kitchen organization and time management, portion control
  and food-cost awareness, waste reduction, teamwork and communication.

## Outcomes

By the end of the program you will be able to prepare and season sushi
rice to a consistent professional standard, break down and portion fish
safely and precisely, produce the full classic range from nigiri to
uramaki, present sushi to restaurant standard, organize and run a sushi
station, and work to professional hygiene and HACCP standards.

## Our philosophy

Learn · Practice · Master · Create. Our aim is not simply to teach you how
to make sushi. It is to build the knowledge, technique, discipline and
confidence a professional sushi kitchen demands, so that every piece you
serve reflects real craftsmanship.
