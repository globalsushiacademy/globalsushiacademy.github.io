A hands-on introduction to traditional Japanese sushi. In a single session
you prepare the rice, learn to handle a knife safely, and roll and shape
the classics yourself, from hosomaki to nigiri.

**Start dates.** There is no fixed schedule. The course runs on demand and
starts once the minimum number of participants is reached. Contact us to
register your interest.

## What you will do

Nine short stages, all practical. You learn the basics of traditional
Japanese sushi preparation, from seasoning the rice to shaping and
presenting each style yourself.

**01 Introduction to Sushi** — Japanese sushi culture and traditions, the
most popular types of sushi, and the essential sushi ingredients.

**02 Sushi Rice** — Selecting the right rice, cooking sushi rice, preparing
traditional sushi seasoning, and correct texture and handling.

**03 Basic Knife Skills** — Safe and professional knife handling, basic
cutting techniques, preparing vegetables and fillings, and an introduction
to fish preparation.

**04 Hosomaki — The Thin Roll** — The classic starting point: rice, one
filling, nori. You practise spreading the rice evenly, placing the filling
correctly, rolling firmly and cutting clean, even pieces.

**05 Futomaki — The Thick Roll** — Futomaki literally means "thick roll": a
larger traditional roll holding seasoned sushi rice and several fillings
wrapped in nori. You learn to balance different ingredients and build a
well-shaped, evenly filled roll.

**06 Uramaki — The California Roll** — Inside out, and the best known roll
in the world. Rice sits on the outside, nori surrounds the filling. The
classic California roll is made with crab or imitation crab, avocado and
cucumber, usually finished with sesame seeds.

**07 Temaki — The Hand Roll** — A cone of nori filled with sushi rice and
your choice of fillings. Unlike maki, temaki is hand-rolled and served
immediately, without cutting.

**08 Nigiri** — The purest form: rice, hand-shaped, with a single topping.
You learn the basic technique for shaping the rice and placing the topping
correctly.

**09 Presentation** — Arranging sushi neatly on a plate, correct portion
placement, garnishing, using wasabi and pickled ginger, and creating a
balanced, attractive plate.

## What you take home

By the end of the session you will understand the fundamentals of sushi
preparation and be able to make several popular styles on your own:
seasoned sushi rice, basic knife skills, hosomaki, futomaki, uramaki,
temaki, nigiri, and basic sushi presentation.
